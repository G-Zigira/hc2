Purpose

Accelerating Rwanda's shift to clean transport.

HC Energy Mobility exists to accelerate Rwanda's transition to clean transportation by building, owning and operating the nation's most extensive electric vehicle charging infrastructure, making clean energy accessible, reliable and convenient for every Rwandan driver, in every province, every day.

🎯

Our Mission

To make electric vehicle ownership practical and affordable for every Rwandan by building the nation's first nationwide DC fast-charge network, placing 26 charging stations across all 5 provinces at trusted Source Oil Company forecourts, so that no Rwandan is more than a short drive from a fast charger.

🔭

Our Vision

A Rwanda where every motorist drives electric, where the energy powering transport is generated locally, priced fairly, and keeps foreign exchange inside Rwanda. We envision HC Energy Mobility as East Africa's model for privately-led EV infrastructure: replicable, scalable and profitable.

The Network

From Kigali to every province.

Rwanda is ready for cleaner and more affordable mobility, but EV adoption cannot scale without reliable charging infrastructure. We are closing that gap by deploying fast chargers at trusted forecourt locations.

Kigali City

Where the network begins. Fast chargers at trusted forecourts give the capital's drivers the confidence to switch to electric.
